## 📝 Part 2: Critical Reflection on AI Tool Limitations

While the AI platform effectively consolidated multi-page disaster risk assessments into succinct structural themes, it displayed significant vulnerabilities in data precision:

* **Inability to Handle Scale:** The tool severely underreported the human impact of the 2024 Shear Line flooding, missing the scale by nearly a million affected citizens.
* **Project Life-Cycle Conflation:** The AI frequently conflates an administrative endorsement (like a NEDA endorsement) or a planned target with actual, on-the-ground project completion by the DPWH. 

**Policy Warning:** For regional policy research in Davao, relying blindly on automated summaries introduces severe risk. It risks presenting idealized progress to decision-makers, masking ongoing infrastructural delays and overstating the region's actual climate resilience. Manual human auditing remains a mandatory requirement before any brief moves to the policy phase.
